About Us

Based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, Little Lemon is a family owned Multicultural restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist.

The chefs draw inspiration from Malaysian, Chinese, and Thai culture and have a menu of 12-15 items that they rotate seasonally.

The restaurant has a rustic and relaxed atmosphere with moderate prices, making it a popular place for a meal any time of the day.

Little Lemon is owned by MR ZAFARSHOH, who moved to the Malaysia to pursue their shared dream of owning a restaurant.

To craft the menu, MR ZAFARSHOH relies on family recipes and his experience as a chef in Malaysia.

MR ZAFARSHOH does all the marketing for the restaurant and led the effort to expand the menu beyond classic Malaysian to incorporate additional cuisines from the South Asian region.
